The problem

Finding legislation is not the same as knowing what applies.

Businesses can face a huge volume of EU and national rules. The difficult part is connecting those rules to a specific product, activity, market or planned change — especially when requirements differ between countries.

Too much information

Legal sources can be extensive, technical and difficult to filter to the rules that matter for one business situation.

Country differences

A business operating across Europe may need to understand where national requirements add to or differ from EU-level rules.

Hard to act on

Knowing that a regulation exists is only the start. Teams need to understand what to investigate, prepare, compare or verify next.

How ReguLens works

Start with your business — not a regulation number.
